# Visualization Inputs

These files are the saved states and current-sweep arrays used by the final thesis visualization scripts:

- `scripts/four_panel_currents_figure.py`
- `scripts/current_panels_two_cases.py`
- `scripts/cross_section_currents_two_cases.py`

They are kept separate from `data/example_states/` because their purpose is to reproduce the final figure aesthetics rather than to serve as minimal simulation examples.
